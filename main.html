Karur is a centrally located city and the administrative headquarters of the Karur district in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
Situated along the scenic banks of the
Amaravathi
Kaveri
Noyyal river
it is an ancient city with a heritage dating back over 2,000 years to the Tamil Sangam period. Historically serving as the inland capital for the early Chera kings, it was known as Karuvoor or Vanji and evolved into a flourishing ancient trading center where even Roman coins have been excavated. Today, Karur is globally recognized as the "Textile City of India" or the "Home Textile Capital", famous for exporting premium handlooms, cotton fabrics, and mosquito nets to major international retailers.
